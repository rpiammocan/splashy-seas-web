Splashy Seas is a kid-friendly browser game where players swim through colorful ocean scenes, collect friendly sea animals for points, and reach the scene prize at the end.

## Gameplay

- Choose from Mermaid, Scuba Guy, Friendly Torpedo, or Mermacorn.
- Choose one of six ocean scenes.
- Choose Easy, Medium, or Hard difficulty.
- Each scene has two assigned animal collectibles.
- **Swim into an animal to collect it and earn 1 point.**
- Animals that pass by without being collected do not award points.
- There is no lives/health system in the collection version.
- Each round lasts 45 seconds.
- After the timer expires, swim into the scene prize to finish.
- The final screen shows how many animals were collected.

## Controls

### Keyboard

- `Up Arrow` / `W` — swim up
- `Down Arrow` / `S` — swim down
- `Esc` — return home
- `P` — pause
- `M` — toggle sound

### Touchscreen

- Tap menu choices to select a player, scene, and difficulty.
- During gameplay, touch the water and drag up or down to swim.
- Use the on-screen **HOME** button to return to the menu.
- Use **SOUND ON / SOUND OFF** to control audio.

## Sound on iPhone and iPad

If the game is running but you cannot hear sound, check **Silent Mode** first. On iPhone, Splashy Seas game audio may be muted while Silent Mode is enabled.

1. Turn Silent Mode off.
2. Raise the media volume.
3. Close and reopen Splashy Seas if necessary.
4. Tap **Tap to Enable Sound** when prompted.

## Add Splashy Seas to the iPhone or iPad Home Screen

1. Open Splashy Seas in **Safari**.
2. Tap the **Share** button.
3. Choose **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Confirm the name **Splashy Seas**.
5. Tap **Add**.
6. Launch the game later from its Home Screen icon.

## Scenes

- Arctic Waters — Polar Bear and Narwhal
- Tropical Reef — Shark and Crab
- Coral Kingdom — Jellyfish and Octopus
- Seagrass Meadow — Squid and Turtle
- Pirate Cove — Shark and Octopus

Each scene retains its own background, music, collectibles, and finish prize.
